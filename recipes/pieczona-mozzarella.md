# Backed Mozzarella

**Serves:** 4
**Time:** 10 min
**Tags:** fast, italian

## Ingredients

- 1 package of mini mozzarella
- 2 teaspoons of basil
- 150g smoked bacon in slices
- 1 ripe avocado
- 250g cherry tomatoes
- 100g green olives
- 2 tablespoons of olive oil
- 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
- salt
- pepper

## Instructions

1. Wash and cut the cherry tomatoes in half
2. Peel and dice the avocado into large cubes
3. Dust the mozzarella with basil and wrap with bacon
4. Bake the bacon-wrapped mozzarella for 12 minutes in an oven preheated to 195°C
5. Mix the remaining ingredients and season with salt and pepper
6. Finally, add the baked bacon-wrapped mozzarella
